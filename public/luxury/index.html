03 — The Brand

What Is
My Custom Beats

My Custom Beats (MCB) is a premium personalized music experience company that crafts bespoke songs and emotional soundtracks for life's most meaningful moments.

Working with world-class composers, vocalists, and producers, MCB transforms guest stories, celebrations, and milestones into one-of-a-kind musical experiences — delivered with the elegance and discretion that luxury hospitality demands.

"We don't create background music. We create the soundtrack to someone's most precious memory."
